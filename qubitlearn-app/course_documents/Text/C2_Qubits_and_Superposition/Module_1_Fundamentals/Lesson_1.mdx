## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

The Bloch sphere provides a geometric visualization of pure single-qubit states on a 3D unit sphere.

$$
|\psi\rangle = \cos(\theta/2)|0\rangle + e^{i\phi}\sin(\theta/2)|1\rangle
$$
