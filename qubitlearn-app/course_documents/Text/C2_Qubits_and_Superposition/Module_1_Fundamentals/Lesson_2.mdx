## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

The Hadamard gate creates quantum superposition with equal 50% measurement probabilities.

$$
H|0\rangle = |+\rangle = \frac{|0\rangle + |1\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad H|1\rangle = |-\rangle = \frac{|0\rangle - |1\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}
$$
