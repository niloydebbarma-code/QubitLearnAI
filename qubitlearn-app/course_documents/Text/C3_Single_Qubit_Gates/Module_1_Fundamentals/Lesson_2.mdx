## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

T gates are non-Clifford resources essential for universal fault-tolerant quantum computation.

$$
S = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & i \end{pmatrix}, \quad T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & e^{i\pi/4} \end{pmatrix}
$$
