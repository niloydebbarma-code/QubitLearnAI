## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

Euler angle rotations around arbitrary Bloch axes parameterized by continuous angles theta.

$$
R_z(\theta) = \exp(-i\theta Z/2) = \begin{pmatrix} e^{-i\theta/2} & 0 \\ 0 & e^{i\theta/2} \end{pmatrix}
$$
