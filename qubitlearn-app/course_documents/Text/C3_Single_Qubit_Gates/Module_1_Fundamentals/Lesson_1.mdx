## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

Pauli gates form the orthogonal basis for single-qubit Hermitian observables and rotations.

$$
X = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad Y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad Z = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}
$$
