## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

Classical computing uses deterministic binary bits. In microscopic physics, quantum information resides in complex vector spaces.

$$
|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle, \quad |\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1
$$
