## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

Wavefunction collapse under projective measurement transforms quantum statevectors into classical probability distributions.

$$
P(0) = |\langle 0|\psi\rangle|^2 = |\alpha|^2, \quad P(1) = |\langle 1|\psi\rangle|^2 = |\beta|^2
$$
