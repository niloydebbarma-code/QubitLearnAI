## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

An n-qubit composite system spans a 2^n dimensional tensor-product Hilbert space.

$$
|\psi\rangle = |q_0\rangle \otimes |q_1\rangle = c_{00}|00\rangle + c_{01}|01\rangle + c_{10}|10\rangle + c_{11}|11\rangle
$$
