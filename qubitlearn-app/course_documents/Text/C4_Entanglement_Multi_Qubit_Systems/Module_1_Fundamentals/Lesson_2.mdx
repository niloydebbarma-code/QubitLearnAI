## Theoretical Architecture & Physical Principles

Maximally entangled bipartite states displaying non-local quantum correlations violating Bell inequalities.

$$
|\Phi^+\rangle = \frac{|00\rangle + |11\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad |\Psi^-\rangle = \frac{|01\rangle - |10\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}
$$
