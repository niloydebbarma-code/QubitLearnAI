# {frontmatter.title}

Welcome to the lesson on **{frontmatter.title}**. In classical computing, a bit is either $0$ or $1$. In quantum computing, we use a *qubit*, which can exist in a superposition of states:

$$
|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle
$$
